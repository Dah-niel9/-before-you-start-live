# Live Research Verdict Regression Baseline

Protected cases: strong positive evidence => TRY; serious documented negative evidence => SKIP; incomplete but useful evidence => MAYBE; thin evidence => NOT ENOUGH RELIABLE EVIDENCE.

These cases must remain unchanged when the structured research layer is added.


## Research identity protection

For unknown opportunities, only evidence that can be tied to the exact requested opportunity may influence the verdict. Generic Nigeria, freelancing, make-money-online, or unrelated-platform pages must not count as evidence.

Protected identity example: OneForma must be evaluated from OneForma-specific evidence; unrelated generic opportunity pages must be ignored.
